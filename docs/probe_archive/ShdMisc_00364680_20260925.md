## Starting point

The guarded body measured 276 words and 138 fnalign edits against 379
instructions. The previous floor note put the residual down to colouring:
the frame was 0x1B0 against retail's 0x1C0, the colour bytes landed in
`$s4`-`$s1` instead of `$s5`-`$s2`, and the table bases were not cached.

## Levers

In order of effect:

- **A 64-byte `ShdQuadVertex` struct** (xyz, uv, q, rgba floats) and plain
  `for` loops over `verts[i]`. Retail's loops jump to the condition, and
  `&verts[i]` is formed once per iteration.
- **`#pragma opt_loop_invariants on`** (push/pop). It hoists the `255.0f`
  bit pattern (`lui 0x437f`, stored with `sw`) and the `andi`/`mtc1` pieces
  of each unsigned conversion. Propagation must stay on: with
  `opt_propagation off` the hoisted constant goes through an FPR and is
  stored with `swc1`.
- **The render-state and draw tables through `(void *)`-cast locals**:
  `renderState = (void *)D_00887300;` and `draw = (void *)D_00887310;`. This
  is the same idiom as `effBlurFilter.c`. Without the cast, propagation
  folds `renderState[0]` back into a `lui`/`lw` at every call. With the
  cast, the bases stay in `$s0`/`$s6`, and the frame grows to the retail
  0x1C0 (`$fp` is saved). The first draw inside `if (flag)` stays direct,
  as in retail.
- **`s32` colour channels** converted with `(f32)(u32)`. As `u32` they
  colour `$s4`-`$s1` with the flag in `$s5`. As `s32` they land in
  `$s5`-`$s2` with the flag in `$s1`. Declaration order had no effect
  (ten orders all measured the same).
- **A block-scoped counter for each loop.** A function-scope `i` is
  allocated `$v0`, ahead of the hoisted invariants. Retail gives the counter
  the register after them (`$a0`, `$a2`, `$t0`). This took the body from 57
  edits to 2.
- **`*(u32 *)ptr` for the raster argument.** Written as `*(s32 *)ptr`, the
  load is scheduled before `li $a0, 1`. This took the body from 2 edits
  to 0.

The last draw's vertex order is (x, x+w, x, x+w). The guarded body had it
as the first two draws' (x, x+w, x+w, x), which was wrong.

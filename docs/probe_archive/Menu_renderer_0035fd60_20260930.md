## Reconstruction and source evidence

This renderer draws animated panels, two eleven-column pip grids, three-row
sprite passes, a scrolling six-row list, selected-item text and footer
sprites. Its exact original menu name is not established, so the address-form
function name remains. The fresh guarded baseline emitted 7,232 bytes and
had 471 aligned instruction edits; the older September 19 source note's
478-edit measurement was historical, not a measurement of current main.

The ABI and behavior audit identified four concrete problems in that attempt:

- The background call passes a real `Vec2f` and an unsigned byte promoted to
  the provider's `s32` alpha parameter. Retail `0035FE60` masks that argument;
  the old signed-byte cast incorrectly extended alpha values above 127
- The solid rectangle contains integer x/y/width/height. Retail truncates x
  at `003615D8` and y at `003615FC`; the old attempt stored float bits instead.
  The existing primitive wrapper copies four words through an `f32 *`
  transport view before its provider interprets them as integers. A real
  integer/float union preserves that existing, unchanged interface
- The scrollbar preserves its fractional origin. Its second and third draws
  use the base plus 165 and the base plus 3 plus the integer scroll fraction.
  There is no intermediate integer rounding or subtract-back by 165
- The label-sprite pass uses the alpha left by the preceding three-row pass,
  not the earlier cursor alpha. Retail overwrites it at `003606A8`, then
  narrows the retained word at `00360734`

Resource snapshots correspond to the loads of the shared pip texture at
`00360048` and `00360BEC`, and the cursor texture at `003604E8`.
The captured cursor RGB survives render-state callbacks; its alpha is reset
for the masking operation. Other palette bytes remain live at each draw.
Typed four-byte palette views retain those real address lifetimes without
caching the colors. The cursor interpolates from a captured old coordinate
pair, while resource reads precede the remaining scalar draw arguments.

Scoped `opt_lifetimes on` and `opt_loop_invariants on` preserve the retail
saved-register lifetimes and grid-opacity preheaders. Natural declaration
order, separate row/label opacity snapshots, and the row-index product in
the vertical displacement close the remaining differences. There is no
inline assembly, ordinary-memory `volatile`, artificial padding, fake ABI
or altered public provider definition.

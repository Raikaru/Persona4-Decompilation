## What the old draft had wrong

- The shuffle is a Fisher-Yates walk: `for (k = n - 1; k > 0; k--)` with
  `c = (k + 1) * (rand12 / 4096.0f)`. The draft set `k = c - 1` after each swap.
- Each swap moves whole 0xFB0-byte card records. A 0xFB0-byte struct
  assignment (`ShuffleCardRecord`, 0x3EC words) is exactly retail's
  0x1F6-iteration lw/lw/sw/sw copy loop; no hand-written copy loop is needed.
  The three record temporaries sit at sp+0x80, sp+0x1030 and sp+0x1FE0 when
  declared row-0 swap, fallback swap, row-1 swap.
- Below six cards the deck is three pairs of rows (`i += 2`, rows `i` and
  `i + 1`); otherwise three single rows, and that branch reloads the count
  field for its `k` start.

## Lever

Retail computes both row products (`mult` + `mult1`), then the counter, then
the two row bases. That is `first = i * n; second = (i + 1) * n;` before the
`k` loop, indexing `cards[first + k]`, with `#pragma opt_loop_invariants on`
hoisting `cards + first * 0xFB0` into the preheader. Explicit row pointers
(`row0 = &cards[i * n]`) leave 10 words (the second product is forwarded to
its use); `(cards + first)[k]` also matches; without the pragma the indexed
form costs 214-274 words.

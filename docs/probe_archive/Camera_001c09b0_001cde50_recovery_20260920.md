Both functions are integrated as ordinary C in the September 20 continuation.
The source inspiration is Persona 3 FES donor commit
`04d95e22710aa185a77d6673080160746d8c7397`: `btlCameraFrameActionTarget`
(`002a7390`) and `FUN_002b41e0`. The donor was inspected as a source hypothesis;
the acceptance evidence below is fresh compilation against the Persona 4 USA
retail executable, not a claim of a new donor-game compiler run.

Each complete resolved instruction stream equals retail. All 62 and 45

No assembly transcription, artificial stores or instruction padding is added.

## Source and interfaces

Each frame is a real 28-byte position/quaternion record, and the interpolation
cache is a real 40-byte pair of quaternions plus its scalar and flag. The old
decompiler fragments separated parts of these values into unrelated scalars.
The recovered copies initialize the complete records consumed by
`001bd560`, `001ec2b0`, `003dcc70`, and the final `001bac20` frame submission.
Quaternion operations use their actual typed arguments and float return values.

The first callback keeps the target-height calculation, angular limit and
small-angle fallback, both lens calls, horizontal normalization and 25-unit
height clamp. The second keeps the unit-center transform, radius calculation,
angular interpolation, 600-unit distance lower bound and horizontal offset.
The polynomial evaluation order and multiply-add structure are preserved by
the compiled output. Both update the same frame queue and duration fields as
retail. The separate argument order and return contracts in other guarded
camera callers were reconciled where affected; those drafts remain guarded.

The complete retail, Ghidra and IDA bodies were inspected alongside the actual
pose-copy, center, angular-distance and queue providers. The native test driver
uses the recovered camera and queue bodies with controlled geometry backends;
it does not emulate the game or the PlayStation 2 floating-point hardware.
Saved GCC and Clang runs each passed 8,192 scenarios and 2,806,418 checks with
undefined-behavior traps enabled and the project's aliasing assumptions made
explicit. Their source, executable and object hashes remain sealed in
`build/first-party-resume-continue/worker3-p3camera/native-v2/`.

## Integration repair and final verification

The interrupted worker's candidate objects were exact, but its prepared text
patch joined each closing brace to the next `// FUN_...` marker and damaged
several unrelated UTF-8 comments through a Windows-code-page conversion. Those
text defects were repaired before integration. The final objects are identical
to the sealed exact proposal objects; all original comments outside the
recovery and every function marker are preserved.

This distinction matters: the old proposal verification scanned 107 functions
because it missed the two joined markers. The final current-source verifier
scans **109 functions: 94 MATCH / 15 ASM**, retaining `001c0e40` and `001ce390`
as independently tracked neighbors. Scoped source lint reports zero findings
and `git diff --check` passes.

Final evidence is under `build/first-party-resume-active/camera-integration/`:
`preparation.json` records the text repairs and marker sets; `current/proof.json`
records the fresh live-owner objects, every resolved relocation, sibling and
data comparison; `current/verify.json` is the official scoped verifier report.
`camera_integration.py current` repeats the sealed-source comparison without
reapplying a patch. Historical donor/probe receipts remain untouched.

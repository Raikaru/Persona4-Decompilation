# Persona-menu renderer recovery

`src/Camp/cmpPersona.c::func_00135dc0` is active C and matches all 4,576
bytes of its retail window. All 16 other functions in the owner retain their
matching instruction images. No shared provider is changed.

## Source and behavior

The fresh guarded baseline emitted 4,428 bytes with a `0xA0` frame. Retail
uses a `0xB0` frame, one shared eight-byte position at `sp+0xA0`, and one
complete RGBA snapshot at `sp+0xAC`. The old draft split the position into
scalars through most phases and retained only three color channels.
Complete records recover those stack objects and the original draw sequence.

The initializer establishes a 120-entry, 10-by-12 grid at `0x64`, an
84-entry, 7-by-12 grid at `0x9C4`, 28 animation records at `0x1054`, and
36 backdrop records at `0x1594`. Grid entries are 20 bytes; animations are
48 bytes. The menu's 23 sprite pointers begin at `0x1C54`; its allocation
ends at `0x1CC4`. The color table has eight four-byte records, bounded by
the next table at `0x005EB560`.

Two draft behaviors required correction:

- The secondary grid preserves its base alpha across every cell. The old
  draft overwrote that base with each faded result, including its opaque
  palette-3 path, incorrectly accumulating the fade
- The live pulse timer is read after the position getter returns, at
  `0x001369FC`. The draft sampled it before that callback

Per-grid and per-row lifetimes keep resource handles, colors, style bytes,
widths, heights and opacity snapshots coherent across rendering callbacks.
The font style is a genuine signed byte. The native byte-opacity declaration
of `func_00355410` from the preceding recovery is preserved. Local declarations
also follow the established unsigned name-handle return, byte portrait-alpha
setter, and texture-pointer quad interface.

`opt_pulloutconstants off` and `opt_propagation off` remain scoped to this
function. Their removal changes 68 and eight aligned instruction edits,
respectively. An earlier scalarization setting was removed after the complete
records made it unnecessary. No physical-register binding, dummy lifetime,
volatile ordinary memory or ordinary-instruction assembly is used.

## Literal recovery closed the final two words

The final residual was a move/float-load swap in the secondary grid fade call.
The inferred globals are actually the shared float literals:

| Value | Address | Exact bytes, little endian |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `0.6f` | `0x00761260` | `9A 99 19 3F` |
| `0.3f` | `0x0076163C` | `9A 99 99 3E` |
| `1.5707964f` | `0x00761594` | `DB 0F C9 3F` |

All 81 direct GP references to these entries in the retail function-window
span are floating loads, with no direct stores or address takes. Using the
actual literals restores the call argument schedule and emits three distinct
four-byte `.lit4` sections. Their addresses and contents independently match
retail. The 0.3f spelling alone closes the two-word residual.

What closed it:
- The 1/255 factor is a pooled literal. Writing `(1.0f / 255.0f) * x`
  gives retail's `lwc1 $f0, lit; mul.s $f0,$f0,$f1` with the conversion in
  $f1. The old "stack-store rotation floor" came from reading D_0076129C as
  a global.
- The bltz/srl/andi/or conversion sequence is just `(f32)` of an unsigned
  value. `(f32)(u32)byte` works for the colour bytes, and plain `(f32)`
  of the u16 fadeIn/fadeOut fields and of u32 count produces it natively.
  There is no need to expand it by hand.
- The colour word is copied with `rgba.rgba = param->color.rgba` (union
  u32/u8[4]). A 4-byte struct copy uses lwc1/swc1, and an unaligned u8
  struct copies bytewise.
- An inline `btlEplRgbaToV4(V4 *, const u8 *)` converts all four bytes. In
  case 0 the source then overwrites `.v[3] = 1.0f`, so the dead fourth
  conversion leaves only its `lbu; bltz` behind, as in retail.
- The blend uses the temporaries shape from matched func_001b7e70
  (btlMain.c): a0..a2 = src*inv, b0/b1 = dst*scale, b2 = dst[2]
  unscaled, and `blend.v[2] = a2 + b2 * scale`. No pragma is needed here.
  Retail's first two adds stay unfused, and only the third becomes
  mula/madd. Rejected: fully fused expressions (156-160 words), all
  products as temps (162), a loop (not unrolled), `+=` into blend (175),
  and every pragma in the sweep.
- Declare `color` before `blend` so color takes sp80 and blend sp70.

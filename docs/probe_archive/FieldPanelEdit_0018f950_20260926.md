# func_0018f950 (code1_0018.c): field panel editor task, matched 2026-09-26

Window 1872 B (1864 B of code), frame 0x130, six-case jump table. The
guarded draft sat at 328 words / 156 fnalign edits and was rewritten.
These levers took it to MATCH:

- **Pad reads go through the pad record struct.** Retail reloads the pad
  repeat word (`lui`/`lhu` of 0x8C0252) at the start of every cursor
  group. It CSEs the word only inside an if/else-if pair. With the file's
  `extern u16 D_008C0252[]`, b210 keeps one load across the `sw` stores to
  the work area. MWCC treats an unsized extern array as unaliased by
  pointer stores. A struct member, or a sized array too large for .sdata,
  is reloaded. The pad globals are fields of 0x4A-byte records at
  0x8C0240 (see func_00452760's memsets):
  `extern PadStatus D_008C0240[2]` with `.trigger` at +0xE and `.repeat`
  at +0x12. 0x8C0298 is `D_008C0240[1].trigger`. This lever alone moved
  328 to 139 words. It should also close the case-4 reload residual in
  func_0018e810.
- **The 86-byte shape copy is a struct assignment.**
  `shape = D_005F5AC0[n]` of a `{ s16 data[0x2B]; }` gives retail's
  lh/sh loop.
- **The format string is the literal `"%d-%d"`.** It is pooled in .sdata
  at gp-0x5FD8.
- **The two frame colours are static `PanelColor` records copied into
  locals just before each func_0014def0 call.** The locals are declared
  `icon` then `board`: MWCC gives the earlier declaration the higher
  slot, and retail has board at 0x128 and icon at 0x12C. Measured
  alternatives:
  - block-scoped initialised locals: 4 words, the slots swapped;
  - nested blocks or an inline helper: 4;
  - `u8[4]` arrays: 149 to 171;
  - initialised at function scope: 303.
- **Prototypes follow the callees' real parameter order.**
  - func_0014def0 interleaves ints and floats. Its definition order puts
    `move $a3` / `mov.s` x3 / `move $t0` in retail's sequence.
  - func_0017d1f0 takes its trailing s32 after the three floats.
  - All three draw helpers take the vertex buffer (D_007966D0) as a
    pointer. An `(s32)` argument makes b210 hoist the address into a
    saved register.
  - func_0018f7b0's last parameter is s16. Retail passes the field with
    `lh`, and the callee only stores it as a byte.
  - memcpy has its standard `(void *, const void *, u32)` prototype.

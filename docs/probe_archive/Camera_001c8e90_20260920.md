## Recovered storage and contracts

Eight real three-float vectors, three quaternions, a two-float horizontal
vector and the existing 40-byte interpolation cache replace the padded
decompiler stack overlay. The provider now explicitly accepts two complete
`P4CameraFrame` outputs. Both callers use that same interface. The side-camera
caller already had full poses; the 001c9750 wrapper now declares two poses
instead of scalar anchors surrounded by byte gaps. Both callers retain every
instruction and relocation.

The routine first writes the current camera pose to the first output. It
then constructs the adjusted quaternion and writes all three adjusted-position
components to the second output. The unit-center helper uses its existing
`BtlUnit *, BtlUnit *, RwV3d *` contract. Quaternion rotation uses the actual
floating angle and integer combine-mode argument order.

## Semantic reconstruction

The retail assembly and both decompiler exports establish several corrections
to the old guarded draft. Actor height comes from its scaled center, and both
center heights contribute to the full framing height before their ground-plane
copies are cleared. The direction's Z coordinate subtracts the target center,
not the actor's saved Z value. The initial look-at uses the full framing height;
the focus and later side-projection calculation use its separately retained
half-height.

Angle limiting uses a complete cache and the sine polynomial already recovered
in neighboring cameras. The polynomial retains the unsquared angle and its
cube. Its XYZ accumulation is staged, while its W component remains a sum of
two products. The endpoint cases, near-zero flag, small-angle rotation and
ordered comparisons are preserved.

The alternate-side search uses the original half-height in its vertical
projection and retains the first-versus-second angle choice. The final camera
Y coordinate is focus Y plus direction Y; the old draft incorrectly used the
alternate probe's Y. The 450-unit distance floor, original focus stores and
half-width lateral offset all match retail.

The first complete-object reconstruction was 2,228 bytes with 19 differing
words, all from a two-register allocation swap. Reusing the full-height
variable for the later extent value closed those differences. No new pragma,
padding, volatile access, dummy effect, or assembly was introduced.

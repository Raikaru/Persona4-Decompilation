# Combination header: exact `func_00325450`

`func_00325450` in `src/Event/Fcl/y_fclCombineDraw.c` is the largest function
in the unit: 4012 instructions in a 16064-byte window, with a nine-way jump
table (`jtbl_00749590`). It now compiles to MATCH from C with every relocation
resolved.

The old guarded body was about 670 alignment edits. Its notes argued about
hoisting `87.0f + spD0` and `2.0f + spD4` by hand. The new body was generated
mechanically from the m2c output and then corrected in three places. It was
exact on the first compile after those fixes, with no shaping needed.

## Contract

- The signature is `(u8 *, s32 mode, s32 flag)`. `mode` is narrowed in place
  (`dsll32 $s0,$s0,24`) for the `!= 2` test and the switch. `flag` stays raw in
  `$s2` because it goes straight into `func_002b77d0`.
- `func_002b77d0` and `func_00329310` are declared at block scope with an
  `s32` mode, so the raw flag needs no narrowing. The shared headers keep `s8`.
- `FclVec2 pos = func_002b2970(18.0f, 14.0f)` and the six
  `FclByte4 cNNN = func_002b2a60(..)` colours are declaration initializers.
  That gives retail's temporary-then-copy (0xC0 to 0xD0, 0x114.. to 0x12C..).
  The later `!= 2` reassignments write in place.
- One named `FclVec2 pt` (0xC8) receives every `func_002b2970` result, and
  `pt.x` / `pt.y` feed the chained points in cases 6 and 7. The
  `func_002b2a60(0x8A, 0xCC, 0xFF, 0)` colours stay nested in their
  `func_002b77d0` calls.

## What the m2c-to-C transform needed

1. Every `temp_fNN` was inlined back into its uses. b210's CSE then chooses
   exactly retail's hoists and rematerialisations. The old notes had measured
   these one temporary at a time.
2. Integer literals added to floats (`573 + pos.x`, `0x183`, ...) keep
   retail's runtime `cvt.s.w`.
3. In the tail, retail inlines `func_00317320(arg0, flag, 0.0f)` three times
   (x = 358 + 0). A file-local `static inline fclShowCursorArrow(s32)` above the
   FUN marker reproduces it: `D_00644C90` is loaded before the flag test, and
   each instance gets its own point slot (0xB8/0xB0/0xA8). A plain call to
   `func_00317320` is not inlined by b210.

The only non-MATCH residual on the way was the `func_00329310` flag narrowing
(the `s32` local extern fixes it) and the missing inline tail.

   inside the consuming call:

   - `*(FclDrawColor *)((u8 *)func_002e04e0(h) + 0x79) = func_002b2a60(..)`;

   b210 evaluates the struct-returning arguments first, and computes the

   creation order, then arrays larger than 8 bytes. Arrays go in declaration
   order, earlier ones higher. The three late 8-byte slots (0x160/0x158/0x150)

   the address computation before the call.
5. **Nested temps.** Single-use call temps are nested into the next statement.
   The `u8` flag for `func_00110810(.., flag | 1)` stays a temp.

## The float registers

Retail saves `$f20..$f22`:

- `$f21` = `(f32)0x177` and `$f22` = 70.0f are loaded at the top of *both*
  arms of the first dispatch and used by the later rows. Two reaching
  definitions keep b210 from propagating them: `x = (f32)0x177;
  size = 70.0f;` in each arm (size first) reproduces it. The first row's
  own item size stays a literal (fresh `lui`), as in retail.
- `$f20` = 70.0f for the label, loaded after the name lookup at the first
  row's label call and only `mov.s`-copied by the later rows. That is the
  label size assigned inside the first row's call
  (`shopDrawLabel(labelSize = 70.0f, ...)`) and passed as `labelSize` after.

Measured alternatives: all literals 567 words (b210 merges the label and
item 70.0f values and keeps neither); named locals assigned once at the top
567 (propagated); propagation off 564-583 (hoists 69.0f and 420.0f too);
`labelSize = 70.0f;` as a statement before the call 517 (loads `$f20`
before the colour and name calls). Dropping `size`, or both `size` and `x`,
leaves 30 / 22 words.

A standalone micro-test (three if/else rows around an inlined label) shows
the same b210 behaviour: a constant used in both arms and after the join is
loaded into a saved FPR at the arm tops, while uses inside the first arms
keep fresh `lui` loads.

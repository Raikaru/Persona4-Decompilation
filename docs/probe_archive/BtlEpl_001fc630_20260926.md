## Contract change: func_001fc300 is a static helper of btlEPL.c
All five retail `jal 0x1fc300` sites lie in btlEPL's window: 0x1fc908
(fc630), 0x1fd974 (fd790), 0x1fff8c (fff40), 0x2002bc and 0x20045c
(00200230). func_001fc300 sits directly before func_001fc4a0 in the image. It
now lives in btlEPL.c as `static s64 func_001fc300(u8 *, u8 *)`, with its
matched body and pragmas unchanged, and it is gone from code1_001f.c (156
MATCH / 1 ASM, down from 157 / 1 only because of the move). btlEPL's extern
`s32 func_001fc300(void *, void *)` was the only other declaration, and it
disagreed with the definition. Because the callee is static, b210 knows its
clobber set, so node/i/mode/inv stay in caller-saved registers across the
call and the frame is 0xF0 as in retail. A non-static definition in the same
unit is not enough (measured). tools/build.py: both SHA-1 OK, 594 objects.

## Levers (see also BtlEpl_001fec00_20260926.md)
- Types: BtlEplUnitParam (select bytes at 0x00 for func_001fc300, a
  BtlEplFadeParam at 0x0C, rotate 0x18, scale 0x1C) and BtlEplUnit (pos
  BtlEplV3 at 0x54, startColor V4 at 0x60, color V4 at 0x70, next at 0xA6C).
  With `node->...` members the addresses fold into $4 offsets. Separate
  `start`/`cur` pointer temps cost addiu's.
- `node->pos = pos` is a BtlEplV3 struct copy (three loads, then three
  stores). The fallback path copies from arg0 element by element.
- `vec[0] = vec[1] = vec[2] = ...` gives retail's z/y/x store order.
- The blend has the func_001b7e70 temporaries shape with four components:
  a0..a3 = src * (1 - scale), b0..b2 = dst * scale, b3 = dst[3], and
  `cur[3] = a3 + b3 * scale`.
- `#pragma opt_dead_assignments off` keeps the four colour conversions ahead of
  the products, in registers $f9/$f3/$f8/$f7 (217 words without it).
- `#pragma opt_loop_invariants on`, with `(1.0f - scale)` written inline in
  the products rather than as a pre-loop local, hoists `i = 0`, then the mode
  andi, then 1 - scale, as retail does. A pre-loop `inv` local puts the
  subtraction first (6 words).
